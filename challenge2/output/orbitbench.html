01 / A measurable design decision

The search evaluates 101 duty-cycle values against eight combinations of solar power, eclipse duration, and battery capacity: 808 simulations. It maximizes modeled payload duty while satisfying energy supply, reserve, and per-orbit sustainability checks.

Tradeoff: reducing payload duty preserves modeled energy reserve at the cost of payload operating time. The mission owner must decide whether the resulting duty meets the payload objective; a feasible energy budget alone cannot answer that question.

04 / What is developed locally

Delivered source: energy solver, input validation, stress search, requirement evaluation, tests, and offline evidence export. The team can inspect, modify, and rerun this software without a paid API or cloud service.

Proposed next capability: instrument adapters and a university-operated validation bench. Programmable power supplies, loads, calibration instruments, and physical EPS hardware remain external dependencies until verified suppliers and lab access are established.

TRL assessment: provisional TRL 2 → candidate TRL 3 for the software concept only, subject to reviewer acceptance of the proof-of-concept evidence. TRL 4 needs laboratory validation. No TRL change is claimed for a spacecraft or EPS hardware.
