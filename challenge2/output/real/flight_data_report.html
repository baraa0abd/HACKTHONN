Traceability and use

Source: BIRDSOpenSource/EPS_dataset at pinned commit. The run verifies each workbook hash against the local manifest. Operational sheets are analyzed; TSURU laboratory “Test” sheets are excluded. Incomplete rows are counted and rejected.

Equations: sum(max(0, V_mV × I_mA / 1,000,000)) across five faces; trapezoidal integration over recorded timestamps. The original dataset authors define positive battery current as discharge and negative as charge.

Engineering decision: these recordings can train health rules and provide measured EPS envelopes. They do not contain the new mission's payload load, attitude, continuous state of charge, or environment, so a flight design still requires mission-specific telemetry or calibrated hardware-in-the-loop tests.
